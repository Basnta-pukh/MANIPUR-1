MANIPUR • BISHNUPUR

Loktak Lake.

Discover Manipur's iconic freshwater lake, famous for its floating phumdis and unique landscape.

About Loktak Lake

Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India and one of Manipur's most remarkable natural attractions. The lake is famous for its floating masses of vegetation called phumdis.

📍 Location

Bishnupur District, Manipur

🌿 What makes it special?

  • Floating phumdis across the lake
  • Beautiful views and peaceful surroundings
  • Close to Keibul Lamjao National Park
  • Famous Sendra viewpoint
  • Boating and sightseeing opportunities

🚗 Getting there

Loktak Lake is approximately 48 km from Imphal and can be reached by road.

MANIPUR • IMPHAL

Kangla Fort.

Explore the historic heart of Manipur and discover the heritage of Kangla.

About Kangla Fort

Kangla Fort is a historic fortified palace complex in Imphal and one of the most important heritage sites of Manipur. Kangla was the traditional seat of political power of the rulers of Manipur.

📍 Location

Imphal, Manipur

🏯 Why it is special

  • Historic royal capital of Manipur
  • Important archaeological and cultural heritage site
  • Historic palace and fort structures
  • Ancient temples and sacred sites
  • Kangla Sha guardian statues
  • Located in the heart of Imphal

🌿 What you can explore

Visitors can explore historic structures, gardens, archaeological remains, temples, museums and other important parts of the Kangla complex.

📖 Historical importance

Kangla has played a central role in the history of Manipur and served as the traditional capital and seat of the state's rulers for centuries.

MANIPUR • BISHNUPUR

Keibul Lamjao.

Discover the world's only floating national park and the home of the endangered Sangai deer.

About Keibul Lamjao

Keibul Lamjao National Park is a unique protected area located on Loktak Lake in Manipur. It is famous for its floating landscape and is closely associated with the Sangai, Manipur's brow-antlered deer.

📍 Location

Bishnupur District, Manipur

🦌 What makes it special?

  • Unique floating wetland ecosystem
  • Home of the Sangai deer
  • Located on Loktak Lake
  • Rich wetland biodiversity
  • One of Manipur's important natural heritage sites

🌿 What you can explore

Visitors can experience the unique wetland landscape, observe the natural surroundings and learn about the Sangai and the ecosystem of Loktak Lake.

🚗 Getting there

The national park is located in the Bishnupur area of Manipur and can be reached by road from Imphal.

MANIPUR • UKHRUL

Shirui Hills.

Explore the beautiful home of the rare Shirui Lily.

About Shirui Hills

Shirui Hills are located in Ukhrul District and are famous for the rare Shirui Lily, the state flower of Manipur. The hills offer beautiful mountain scenery and are an important natural attraction.

📍 Location

Ukhrul District, Manipur

🌸 What makes it special?

  • Home of the rare Shirui Lily
  • Beautiful mountain landscapes
  • Rich natural environment
  • Popular trekking destination

🥾 What you can explore

Visitors can explore the hills, enjoy panoramic views and experience the unique natural environment of Ukhrul.

MANIPUR • KANGPOKPI

Sadu Chiru Waterfall.

Experience one of Manipur's scenic natural waterfalls.

About Sadu Chiru

Sadu Chiru Waterfall is a scenic waterfall surrounded by green hills and forests. Manipur Tourism describes it as having three waterfall spots and highlights it as a tourist destination near the Tiddim Road.

📍 Location

Near Tiddim Road, Manipur

💧 What makes it special?

  • Three waterfall spots
  • Beautiful green surroundings
  • Popular nature destination
  • Scenic hiking experience

🌿 What you can explore

Enjoy the surrounding forests, streams, hills and waterfall scenery.

MANIPUR • UKHRUL

Khangkhui Lime Caves.

Travel deep into one of Manipur's fascinating prehistoric limestone cave systems.

About Khangkhui Lime Caves

Khangkhui Lime Caves are prehistoric limestone caves located in Ukhrul District. Manipur Tourism notes that excavations have found evidence connected with Stone-Age habitation.

📍 Location

Ukhrul District, Manipur

🪨 What makes it special?

  • Prehistoric limestone cave
  • Important archaeological interest
  • Interesting cave formations
  • Natural underground environment

🔎 What you can explore

Discover the cave environment and learn about its geological and archaeological significance.

MANIPUR • SENAPATI

Dzuko Valley.

Discover a spectacular green valley on the Manipur– Nagaland border.

About Dzuko Valley

Dzuko Valley is a scenic valley on the border of Manipur and Nagaland. It is known for its green landscapes, flowers and trekking opportunities.

📍 Location

Senapati District, Manipur

🏔️ What makes it special?

  • Beautiful green valley
  • Famous Dzuko Lily
  • Mountain landscapes
  • Popular trekking destination

🥾 What you can explore

Visitors can experience the valley's landscapes, flowers and trekking routes.

MANIPUR • IMPHAL

Ima Keithel.

Experience the famous women-run market at the heart of Imphal.

About Ima Keithel

Ima Keithel, meaning Mother's Market, is a famous market in Imphal where women run the stalls. It is an important cultural and commercial landmark of Manipur.

📍 Location

Imphal, Manipur

🛍️ What makes it special?

  • Women-run market
  • Important cultural landmark
  • Traditional goods and local products
  • Located in central Imphal

🧺 What you can explore

Explore local products, handloom items, food, household goods and the lively market environment.
